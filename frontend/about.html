About us

Better food. Stronger farms.

AgriMarket is an online marketplace that connects smallholder farmers directly with the people who eat their produce: households, restaurants and small shops.

Many farmers lose a large part of their income to middlemen and to produce that spoils before it reaches a buyer. Many buyers, on the other hand, cannot easily find fresh produce at a fair price. We built AgriMarket to fix both problems in one simple place.

Our mission

To give every farmer a fair market for their harvest, and every buyer a trusted way to source fresh, local food.

How it works

1

Farmers list their harvest

Farmers create a free account, add their farm and list produce with prices and stock.

2

Buyers browse and order

Buyers search the catalogue, fill a cart and place one order with delivery details.

3

Fresh produce is delivered

Farmers confirm the order and the produce is delivered, paid for by M-Pesa or cash.

What we stand for

🤝 Fair prices

Farmers set their own prices and keep more of every sale.

🌱 Freshness first

Direct orders mean produce spends less time in storage and transit.

✅ Verified farmers

Our team checks farms before they receive the verified badge.

📱 Simple to use

Clear pages that work on a phone, a tablet or a computer.
